    What was built

    Four parts of the system, as described in my internship work.

    • Local RAG pipeline. Ollama handles LLM inference and Pgvector handles semantic retrieval over internal HR policy and employee data.
    • Document intelligence. OCR and automated vector indexing for PDF uploads: data extraction, signature verification and contextual document querying.
    • Workflow backend. A Django REST backend for human-in-the-loop workflows with decision history, error handling and audit trails.
    • Access and APIs. Role-based access and REST APIs connecting a React.js frontend to Postgres, with request/response lifecycles validated.
